About Us

THE REAL ESTATE CORNER

The Real Estate Corner is a premier real estate agency and general contractor based in Bormotoria, Guwahati, Assam. With a perfect 5-star rating and a growing family of satisfied clients, we have built our reputation on seamless transactions, transparent dealings, and a deep understanding of the local property market. Whether you are looking for your dream home, a commercial space, or a profitable investment, our experienced team is committed to making the process smooth and stress-free.

Located conveniently opposite Sunvally Hospital on Purbanchal Path, we proudly serve clients across Guwahati and the wider Northeast India region. Our team also collaborates closely with interior and construction professionals to offer end-to-end property solutions — from finding the right plot to finishing your interiors. We are open six days a week and are always ready to understand your unique needs and fulfill them with speed, professionalism, and care.
